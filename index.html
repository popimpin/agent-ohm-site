Step 3 · Can I trust it?

When it isn't sure, it asks you.

A lot of people have been burned by AI that guesses. When two choices look the same, Agent-Ohm doesn't guess. It stops and asks you on your phone. When it acts in your name, your fingerprint says yes first.

  1. Your files stay on your machines.Your work and its history live on computers in your home, not on our server.
  2. Cloud AI only when you choose.Jobs run on your own computers by default. Call in a cloud AI like Claude or GPT for a hard job, by your choice, for that job.
  3. Your fingerprint before it acts for you.Nothing goes out in your name (a post, an email, a booking) until you approve it on your phone.
  4. Leave anytime, keep everything.Uninstall it and your work is still there, in ordinary files. No account to close, nothing to export.

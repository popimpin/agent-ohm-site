Step 1 · What is it?

Your phone becomes the remote for your computers.

Agent-Ohm's phone app is a home screen. From it you can see which of your computers are on, give them a job, and say yes or no when an agent needs you. It works on mobile data, from anywhere, not just on your home Wi-Fi.

Step 2 · What does it do?

It buzzes when an agent needs you.

Agents get stuck when nobody is at the desk to answer. Agent-Ohm sends the question to your phone instead. It beeps, it buzzes, and the card shows you exactly what it's asking.

Step 3 · Is my phone safe?

Your fingerprint is the key. It never leaves the phone.

The app doesn't come with a master password inside it. It only joins your computers after you say yes at your PC and touch the fingerprint sensor.

  1. Paired by you, at your desk.The phone shows a 6-digit code. You approve it at your PC, then confirm with your fingerprint. Only then can it talk to your computers.
  2. Signed with a key locked in the phone's security chip.Your approvals are signed by a key that stays in the phone's hardware. It can't be copied out.
  3. Android's own lock screen stays in charge.Agent-Ohm never draws over your lock screen. Lock your phone and everything waits for your fingerprint or PIN, the same as always.
  4. Nothing private on the notification.While the phone is locked, the alert only says an agent needs you. The question shows after you unlock.
Step 4 · Is it still my phone?

Yes. Calls, apps and the camera all still work.

Agent-Ohm replaces your home screen, not your phone. Your dialer, messages, camera and every app are still there: swipe up for the full app drawer. Press the home button and your computers are one look away.

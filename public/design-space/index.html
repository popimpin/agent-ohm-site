Step 3 · What if it goes wrong?

Nothing is lost. You can always go back.

Building with an AI means some changes will be wrong. Design Space is built for that.

  1. Undo works.Ctrl+Z steps back through your changes, and Ctrl+Shift+Z steps forward again.
  2. A bad save can't wipe your page.Every save keeps the version it replaced, so you can roll back to it.
  3. Precise changes stay precise.Ask for one part to change, and the agent changes that part, not the whole page.
  4. Your pages are yours.They're saved on your own computer as ordinary files.
